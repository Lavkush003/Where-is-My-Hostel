Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Where Is My Hostel collects, how it is used and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect the username, email address and password information you provide. Passwords are stored using Django's secure password hashing and are never stored in plain text.

We may also receive information you send to us directly, such as messages about a property, a listing request or a support question.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To respond to enquiries and improve the search experience.
  • To understand which features and pages are useful to students and property owners.
  • To protect the platform against misuse, fraud and security risks.

3. Cookies and sessions

The site uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect forms against cross-site request forgery. These cookies support core site functionality. If analytics or additional cookies are introduced later, this policy will be updated before they are used.

4. Sample listings

Some property cards and city guides currently contain sample content for design preview. Sample listings are labelled and are not live offers. You should confirm any property details directly with the owner before making a decision.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us host the website, deliver email or maintain security, but only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

6. Data retention and security

We keep account information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to operate the service. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect data, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your account information by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or operational reasons.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the contact page. Please also read our Terms of Use.
