About the project

Student housing should be easier to find.

Where Is My Hostel is being built to help students across India discover safe and affordable PGs, hostels, private rooms and rental accommodation close to the places they spend their time.

The problem we are solving

Moving to a new city for a course or an exam batch often means piecing together information from messaging groups, notice boards and word of mouth. Students need a clearer way to compare options and understand what is actually nearby.

Our focus is location-first discovery. Instead of browsing an endless list, students will be able to search around coaching institutes, colleges, markets, railway stations and other important landmarks.

What the platform will include

  • Verified PG, hostel and room listings
  • Nearby landmark information with OpenStreetMap
  • Direct enquiries between students and owners
  • Owner tools for managing properties and responses

How the foundation is built

The project uses a modular Django backend, server-rendered templates and a maintainable Tailwind CSS build. Maps and location services are planned around Leaflet, OpenStreetMap, Nominatim and Overpass.
